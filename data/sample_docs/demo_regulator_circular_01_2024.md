# Demo Regulator — Circular No. 01 of 2024 (FICTIONAL SAMPLE DATA)

Subject: Minimum CCTV standards and cash handling in bank branches

Date: 15 March 2024

1. All banks shall install CCTV cameras of at least 5 megapixels at ATM
   vestibules, branch entrances and cash counters.
2. Video records shall be preserved for a minimum of 90 days.
3. Branches in Tier-1 cities shall comply by 31 March 2025; all other branches
   by 31 December 2025.
4. Banks shall submit an implementation plan to the Banking Supervision
   Department within 60 days of the date of this circular.
5. Cash counters shall not hold more than PKR 5 million outside the vault at any
   time.
